Aim:
To implement the Producer-Consumer problem using semaphores and demonstrate deadlock prevention using resource ordering.

Commands Used:

gcc producer_consumer.c -o producer_consumer -pthread
./producer_consumer

gcc deadlock.c -o deadlock -pthread
./deadlock

Observations:

1. Producer inserts items into the shared buffer.
2. Consumer removes items from the buffer.
3. Counting semaphores synchronize producer and consumer.
4. Mutex protects the critical section.
5. Resource ordering prevents deadlock by acquiring locks in the same order.

Four Necessary Conditions for Deadlock:

1. Mutual Exclusion
2. Hold and Wait
3. No Preemption
4. Circular Wait

Deadlock Prevention:

Acquire shared resources in a fixed order to eliminate circular wait.

Conclusion:

Semaphores and mutexes ensure correct synchronization. Resource ordering effectively prevents deadlock.
