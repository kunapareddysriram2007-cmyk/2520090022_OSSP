Aim:
To study process synchronization using wait() and waitpid() and observe zombie processes.

Commands Used:

ps -el
ps -aux
top

Observations:

1. Parent creates two child processes.
2. wait() waits for any child process.
3. waitpid() waits for a specific child process.
4. Zombie process appears when the child exits before the parent calls wait().
5. Zombie process disappears after the parent collects the child's exit status using wait().

Conclusion:

Proper synchronization using wait() or waitpid() prevents zombie processes.
