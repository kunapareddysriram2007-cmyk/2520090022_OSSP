Aim:
To implement job listing and foreground job control.

Commands Used:

gcc skill-17.c -o skill-17
./skill-17

Observations:

1. Background jobs are assigned unique job IDs.
2. The jobs command displays all active jobs.
3. Job ID, PID, command, and status are shown.
4. The fg command selects a job by its ID.
5. waitpid() waits until the selected job finishes.
6. Completed jobs are removed from the active job list.

Conclusion:

The program demonstrates job listing, foreground switching, job monitoring, and process synchronization similar to a basic Unix shell.
